One minute, five steps

Setting the adult's PIN — four digits they say themselves

⭐ New with the PIN at the door. The PIN is how an adult who arrived without a phone opens a request at the tablet. Open the child's card → SafePass tab → Set PIN next to the adult's name.

  1. Ask them to say four digits. Do not choose the digits for them: a PIN chosen for someone is a PIN written on a piece of paper in their bag.
  2. Type the four digits and save. If the screen says those digits are already in use in this center, ask for four different ones — two people cannot share a PIN, or the teacher would see the wrong face at the door.
  3. Tell them what it is for: at the tablet they tap PIN at the door, enter their four digits, and the teacher sees their name and photo and hands the child over face to face. The PIN opens a request — it never opens the door by itself.

No photo on file? The door will ask the teacher to check ID. Take the photo at the desk while the adult is standing in front of you.

Registering a parent and installing the app — one minute

The family should not have to learn anything. They come to you; you do these five things and hand the phone back. Everything below happens on their phone, not on ours.

  1. Register them. Take the photo and the phone number on your own screen — the photo is what a teacher sees at pick-up, so take it here, not "later".
  2. Open the Parent App on their phone. Scan the QR from the parent letter or type the center's link. Nothing is installed from an app store.
  3. Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.
    📷 Temporary drawing. Replace with a real screenshot from the phone.
  4. Enter their number once. The screen says “Enter your registered phone number” and under the box “Use the number Play Academy has on file for pickup.” Type it, tap Continue →. No code is sent and there is no password — registering the number is what activates it.
  5. Wait for the green ✓ and hand the phone back. Their children are on the screen; that is the whole setup. Tell them the only thing they need to remember: two taps a day.

If it says “This number is not registered yet” — the registration in step 1 has not saved, or the number differs by a digit. Fix it on your screen and try again; do not send the family home to "try later".

No smartphone? Skip steps 2–4: they get a personal card with a QR code — the same security, the same one-tap process.
