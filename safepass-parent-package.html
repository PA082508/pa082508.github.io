Letter to families
Subject: Picking your child up is about to get simpler — and safer

Dear parents,

We are adding SafePass: a simple way of recording who brought your child and who took them home. It takes two taps — one at drop-off, one at pick-up — and the teacher confirms each one. For the record, this is a big change: every hand-off is written down at the moment it happens, by the two people who were there.

Why we are doing this: a child is handed from person to person several times a day, and until now that trail lived in memory and on paper. Now it is a record — one that protects your child, and everyone else with them.

One visit to the front desk is all there is to learn: the director registers you and sets up the app on your phone right there — about a minute. The director also generates your personal QR code, which works when your phone is not with you. Most importantly, you set your personal PIN. In an emergency, your PIN is how you authorize your child's release to a person who is not registered. It is also your key to your personal account, where you can check your documents and update some details.

If you have not registered yet, the teacher hands your child over the usual way — in person, face to face, to the adult they know.

— Play Academy
No signature is asked of families for SafePass, and nothing is charged. Activating your phone number is your agreement.
