Letter to families
Subject: Picking your child up is about to get simpler — and safer

Dear parents,

We are adding SafePass: a simple way of recording who brought your child and who took them home. What changes for you is two taps — one when you arrive, one when you collect — and the teacher confirms on their side. What changes for the record is bigger: every hand-off is written down at the moment it happens, by the two people who were there.

Why we are doing it: a child is handed from person to person several times a day, and until now that trail lived in memory and on paper. Now it is a record — one that protects your child first, and everyone else second.

For most families it is one visit to the front desk and nothing else to learn: we register you and set up the app on your phone right there. If you would rather do it yourself from the letter, the steps are inside — one minute, once.

No smartphone? Ask at the front desk for your personal photo card — same safety, same one tap. Taking part is how pick-up works for everyone. While the printed cards are being rolled out, the desk hands out a one-time code for that pick-up instead; the check at the door is the same either way, and a card or code alone never releases a child — the person is confirmed face to face.

— Play Academy
No signature is asked of families for SafePass, and nothing is charged. Activating your phone number is your agreement.
1 · The fast way (most families)

Come to the front desk once. We register you, set up the app on your phone, and you are done — about a minute. From then on: two taps a day.

At the desk it goes one of three ways, and all three end the same:

  • We register you — a photo and your number — and set up the app on your phone right there. You leave with it on your home screen.
  • Or you take your QR letter and finish at home in a minute — the steps are in section 3.
  • Or, if you don't use a smartphone, you get your personal card — same check at the door, same single tap. While the printed cards are being rolled out, the desk hands out a one-time code for that pick-up instead; a card or code alone never releases a child — the person is confirmed face to face.

Why the photo

It is shown to the teacher at pick-up, so they see who is asking for the child before handing them over. It is used for nothing else: not shared, not published, and removed when your child leaves the centre.

Anyone else who may collect your child

Everyone you authorize — including emergency contacts — registers once at the desk: a photo and a phone number (or a card). One minute, once. Grandparents, a neighbour, an older sibling: you decide who, the enrolment paper you signed says so, and the desk records it. Being named on the form is what gives the right; registering is what gives the teacher a face to check at the door.

Who may collect your child is decided at the office, on the enrolment form you signed — teachers cannot add or remove anyone from the room. An adult who is not on that list is not given the child, however familiar they look. That is the one rule worth explaining to your family.
2 · Everyday use — two taps

Dropping off

Open the app and tap your child. The teacher comes to you, takes your child, and taps Accept on their tablet — “Teacher receives the child”, in the app's own words. From that second the child is counted in their room.

Picking up

Tap your child. The teacher sees who is asking and their photo, brings the child, and confirms. Your screen turns green ✓ — that is the receipt, and it is the same confirmation the centre keeps.

Your tap is the request; the teacher's tap is the record. Nothing is signed by pressing early, and nothing is lost by pressing late.

If your phone is dead, forgotten, or not with you

Go to the desk: staff release the child through the paper panel and a one-time code for that pick-up, and the day is recorded afterwards. Nobody is turned away for lack of a phone — and this is the exception, not a second way of doing it.

3 · Setting up yourself from the letter link?

If the desk did not set it up for you — you got the letter with the QR code, or you left in a hurry — this is the whole thing, once. It takes about a minute.

Open it

There is nothing to find in an app store. Scan the QR from your letter, or tap the link the centre sent: SafePass opens in the browser you already have.

Enter your number — once

The screen says “Enter your registered phone number” and, under the box, “Use the number Play Academy has on file for pickup.” Type that number and tap Continue →. No code arrives and there is no password: the desk activated your number when it registered you, so the number itself is the key.

If the number is not recognised, the screen says so in plain words — “This number is not registered yet. Ask Play Academy staff to register your phone, then try again.” That means the desk has not registered it yet, not that anything is broken.

On the same screen there is a second, smaller line: “Given a one-time code? Enter it.” That is for the rare case when staff hand someone a code at the desk — an adult without a smartphone, for example. It is not part of the ordinary way in, and no code is ever sent to you by text.

Questions & answers

Do I have to take part?

Yes — this is simply how pick-up works now, for every family. What is optional is the phone: without a smartphone you use your personal photo card from the desk, with the same check and the same single tap. Nobody is left out, and nobody opts out.

Does it cost anything?

No. Not now and not later — it is part of how the centre keeps its records.

Who can see my child's photo and mine?

Staff of your centre, at the moment of a hand-off. Photos are not shared with anyone, not published, and are removed when your child leaves.

Can my mother pick the child up?

Yes — with two things in place, and both are quick. She must be named on your enrolment form as allowed to collect, and she must register once at the desk: a photo and a phone number, or a card if she has no smartphone. The form gives her the right; the photo is what the teacher checks at the door. Bring her in once and it is done.

What if someone I did not approve comes for my child?

The child stays with the teacher. The teacher calls the desk, and the desk calls you. If you say yes, you can authorize that person right from your phone in a minute — they then collect the child with that one-time note and their photo ID. If you say the person was never sent, the answer at the door is final and the director is told. Either way the door does not open on a guess.

I changed my phone number.

Tell the desk, and they will register the new one. Until they do, the app will say “This number is not registered yet” — the number is your key, and a key that now belongs to someone else is the one thing we cannot leave in place.

Does the app track where I am?

No. It records taps at the door — who handed the child over and when — and nothing about your location.

What if the internet is down at the centre?

Staff fall back to the paper panel, and the day is recorded afterwards. A hand-off is never delayed because a screen is unhappy.

When does this start for my room?

Your director will tell you. One room starts first and the rest follow.

Questions we did not answer here belong to your director — ask any day.
