Letter to families
Subject: Picking your child up is about to get simpler — and safer

Dear parents,

We are adding SafePass: a simple way of recording who brought your child and who took them home. Nothing about your day changes — you still walk in, greet the teacher and take your child. What changes is that every hand-off is written down at the moment it happens, by the two people who were there.

Why we are doing it: a child is handed from person to person several times a day, and until now that trail lived in memory and on paper. Now it is a record — one that protects your child first, and everyone else second.

It takes one visit to the front desk and about two minutes on your phone. Both are described below, step by step. If you would rather not use a phone at all, you do not have to: the desk keeps working exactly as before.

— Play Academy
No signature is asked of families for SafePass, and nothing is charged. Activating your phone number is your agreement.
1 · Register at the front desk — once

The first step happens at the centre, not on the phone. Come to the front desk and the staff will register you: your name, your phone number, and a photo taken right there.

Why the photo

It is shown to the teacher at pick-up, so they see who is asking for the child before handing them over. It is used for nothing else: not shared, not published, and removed when your child leaves the centre.

Anyone else who may collect your child

Grandparents, a neighbour, an older sibling — bring them to the desk once and they are registered the same way. You decide who; the enrolment paper you signed says so; the desk records it.

An adult who is not registered will not be handed the child — even if the teacher recognises them. That is the whole point of the system, and it is the one rule we ask you to explain to your family.
2 · Get the app on your phone

There is nothing to find in an app store. SafePass opens in the browser you already have, and then sits on your home screen like any other app.

4 · Everyday use

Signing in — one screen, and that is all

Enter your phone number — the same one you registered at the front desk. That is the whole sign-in: no code arrives and there is no password. The desk already activated your number when it registered you, so the number itself is the key. You will see your own children and nobody else's.

If the number is not recognised, the screen says so in plain words — “This number is not registered yet. Ask Play Academy staff to register your phone, then try again.” That means the desk has not registered it yet, not that anything is broken.

On the same screen there is a second, smaller line: “Given a one-time code? Enter it.” That is for the rare case when staff hand someone a code at the desk — an adult without a smartphone, for example. It is not part of the ordinary way in, and no code is ever sent to you by text.

Dropping off

Tap your child's name. The teacher accepts, and from that second the child is counted in their room. You are free to go.

Picking up

Tap your child's name. The teacher sees your name and your photo, brings the child, and confirms. Your tap is the request; the teacher's tap is the record.

If your phone is dead, forgotten, or not with you

Go to the desk. Staff can release the child the way they always have, and an adult without a smartphone can be given a one-time code for that pick-up. Nobody is turned away for lack of a phone.

Questions & answers

Do I have to use it?

No. The desk and paper keep working. The app is faster for you and clearer for us, and that is the whole argument.

Does it cost anything?

No. Not now and not later — it is part of how the centre keeps its records.

Who can see my child's photo and mine?

Staff of your centre, at the moment of a hand-off. Photos are not shared with anyone, not published, and are removed when your child leaves.

Can my mother pick the child up?

Yes, once she is registered at the desk. Bring her in once; after that she is on the list you approved.

What if someone I did not approve comes for my child?

The child will not be handed over. The teacher can see who is authorised, and an unregistered adult is not.

I changed my phone number.

Tell the desk, and they will register the new one. Until they do, the app will say “This number is not registered yet” — the number is your key, and a key that now belongs to someone else is the one thing we cannot leave in place.

Does the app track where I am?

No. It records taps at the door — who handed the child over and when — and nothing about your location.

What if the internet is down at the centre?

Staff fall back to paper, exactly as before, and the day is recorded afterwards. A hand-off is never delayed because a screen is unhappy.

When does this start for my room?

Your director will tell you. One room starts first and the rest follow.

Questions we did not answer here belong to your director — ask any day.
