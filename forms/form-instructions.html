How to Complete the CACFP Online Forms

Play Academy Child Care Center — Pearl · Alpha · Ridge

For Parents and Guardians

Our enrollment and nutrition forms are now available online. They are exact copies of the official Ohio CACFP and USDA forms — the only difference is that you can complete and sign them right on your phone, tablet, or computer. No app to install, no printing, no scanning.

Step by step

  1. Open the form packet using the link or QR code provided by your center. You will see a short list of forms with a number next to each one.
  2. Tap the first form. It looks exactly like the paper version you may have seen before — same layout, same wording.
  3. Fill in the fields. As you type, the form helps you:
    • The center name appears in a drop-down list — just pick yours.
    • Phone numbers format themselves automatically: type 2165551234 and it becomes (216) 555-1234.
    • Date fields open a calendar; the signature date is already set to today (you can change it if needed).
    • Check boxes accept only the allowed combination — if a section says “check one,” the form makes sure only one is checked.
  4. Sign with your finger (or mouse) in the signature box. Made a mistake? Tap Clear and sign again.
  5. Tap Submit. A green “✓ Submitted” confirms your form was delivered securely to the center. If anything required is missing, the form tells you exactly what to complete.
  6. Open the next form — it is already half-filled. Your child’s name, birthdate, center, and contact details carry over automatically. Verify them and complete only what is new. The packet page shows a green check mark next to each finished form.
Your privacy: the information you type stays on your own device until you press Submit. Shared details used for auto-filling are erased automatically after 90 minutes, and you can erase them at any moment with the New family button — for example, when completing forms for a second child or when using a tablet at the center.
Need a paper copy? Every form has a Print / Save PDF button. The printout is identical to the official form — suitable for your records or for forms that require a doctor’s handwritten signature.

Which form do I need?

FormWhen to complete it
Special Diet Form Your child needs a food accommodation due to a disability or a special medical/dietary need. The top section is for you; the medical section is completed and signed by your child’s licensed health care provider.
Fluid Milk Substitution You request a non-dairy beverage instead of fluid milk for your child with a special dietary need (no medical statement required).
Infant Meals — Parent Preference Letter Your child is under one year of age — choose who provides formula/breast milk and solid foods.

For Center Staff

Food/Non-Food Cost Worksheet

  • Claim month and year: tap the year next to “YEAR:” — a calendar opens; the year fills in and the claim month is circled automatically. October and September are rejected with a reminder to use the annual inventory packet forms.
  • Receipt rows: dates and amounts load automatically from the receipt database; rows can also be typed by hand. Vendor names suggest themselves from your real supplier list as you type the first letters.
  • Totals (food, non-food, milk gallons by type) recalculate instantly with every change.
  • Submit saves the completed worksheet to the database; Print / Save PDF produces the official-format document for the claim file.

If Submit shows an error

  • Red message naming a field — that required field is empty; complete it and submit again.
  • “Submit failed” — check the internet connection and try again; nothing you typed is lost.
