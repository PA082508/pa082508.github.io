What to bring for your infant's first day

Ages 6 weeks – 18 months
  • Change of clothes — full sets (shirt, shorts/pants, socks — weather appropriate)
  • Diapers
  • Wipes
  • Diaper cream
  • Bottles (3)
  • Baby picture and family photo optional
  • Pacifier optional
Please make sure everything is labeled in permanent marker. If you bring breast milk, add the date it was expressed and the date you bring it. All diaper cream must have a medical form completed before bringing it.
