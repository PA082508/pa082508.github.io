How to Use the QR Code

Complete your CACFP forms on your phone in about 5 minutes
pa082508.github.io/forms/1-data-sources/parent-forms.html
1
Point your phone camera at the code Open the regular Camera app (iPhone or Android) and hold it over the QR code — no special app needed. Tap the link that pops up on the screen.
2
Pick a form You will see a short list. Start with the form that applies to your child — each card explains when you need it.
3
Fill it in Tap each field and type. Your center is in a drop-down list, phone numbers format themselves, and the date is already set to today.
4
Sign with your finger Draw your signature in the box, just like signing for a package. Tap Clear to start over if needed.
5
Tap Submit A green ✓ Submitted means we received it. Open the next form — your details are already filled in for you.

Good to know

  • Camera won’t scan? Type the address under the code into your browser instead.
  • Filling out forms for two children? Tap New family on any form before starting the second child — it clears the shared details.
  • Made a mistake after submitting? Just fill the same form out again — the newest one counts. Tell the front desk so we use the right copy.
  • Need paper? Every form has a Print / Save PDF button, or ask the front desk for a printed copy.
  • Doctor’s section? The Special Diet Form has a section for your child’s health care provider — they can complete and sign it on their own device, or on a printout.
Your privacy: what you type stays on your phone until you press Submit. Shared details used for auto-filling erase themselves after 90 minutes — nothing is left behind on shared tablets.
