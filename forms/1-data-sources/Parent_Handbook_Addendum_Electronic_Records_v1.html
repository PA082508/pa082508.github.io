Electronic records at Play Academy

Play Academy keeps an electronic record of your child's every day. The SafePass system records the exact moment your child is picked up and the exact moment they are returned to you, ensuring that only authorized adults can pick them up. We do this for one reason: your child's safety. This same record also serves as the basis for our nutrition program and government reporting, so what your family signs once fulfills all requirements without additional paperwork.

You don't need to do anything other than what you already do at the entrance: your phone is your key, and a printed card works the same way. Any entry in your child's file can be shown to you — just ask at the front desk.
