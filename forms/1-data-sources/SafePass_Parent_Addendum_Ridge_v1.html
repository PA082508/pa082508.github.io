ADDENDUM: SafePass — Check-In and Check-Out

Effective Monday, August 10 · introduced classroom by classroom, beginning with Red Room (Wickliffe)

Play Academy uses SafePass, the center's check-in and check-out system. Drop-off and pick-up work the way they always have — SafePass simply ensures that every hand-off is properly confirmed and documented, protecting your child, your family, and the center.

Registration. A center administrator registers each authorized adult in person and takes their photo. The photo serves one purpose: at pick-up, it is how the classroom teacher confirms that the adult at the door is the right person. The administrator will help you set up access during registration — it takes about two minutes.

Daily use. At pick-up, the teacher sees the authorized adult's photo on the classroom tablet and confirms their identity face to face before releasing the child. The final decision at the door is always made by a person.

Authorized pickup persons. Children are released only to adults the family has authorized. We strongly encourage every family to authorize at least two adults — a second parent, a grandparent, a trusted friend — so an unexpected circumstance never becomes a problem at the door. To add or remove an authorized adult, speak with your center director; removal takes effect immediately.

Adults without a smartphone. An authorized adult who does not use a smartphone — a grandparent, for example — receives a permanent personal QR code, printed as a card or saved as a picture on any phone. At pick-up, the teacher scans the code, sees that adult's photo, and confirms face to face — the same check as for everyone else. The code is personal, can be replaced at any time, and is deactivated immediately if lost. For a one-time pickup by someone new, speak with the director — a one-time authorization will be arranged.

Your records and signatures. Signing forms electronically is voluntary. If you prefer, you may print any form and sign it by hand, or complete it in person at the office — paper and electronic signatures are equally accepted. Photos are used only for identification at pick-up and required record keeping; when an adult's access is removed, their photo is removed as well.

Compliance. Ohio licensing rules (OAC 5180:2-12-09, continuous supervision; 5180:2-12-18, record keeping) require centers to supervise children continuously and keep accurate attendance records; SafePass is how the center meets these requirements.

Questions are always welcome — please speak with your center director.
