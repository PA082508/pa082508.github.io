PERMISSION TO ADMINISTER MEDICATION
Instructions from a licensed dentist, licensed physician, physician assistant (PA), or advanced practice registered nurse (APRN) are required for administering:
  • Medical foods.
  • Prescription medications, including samples.
  • Non-prescription medicines containing aspirin.
  • Topical preventative products and lotions or non-prescription medications, when the instructions for use exceed or do not match the manufacturer's instructions or are not stored in original container.
