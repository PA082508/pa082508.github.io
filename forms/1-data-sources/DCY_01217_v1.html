Request for Administration of Medication for Child Care

This form is to be completed for each prescription or non-prescription medication that a child needs to receive while in care.
It is not required to be completed for topical products, lotions, or if the medication is required by a health care plan (DCY 01236).

A few questions from the center
For prescription medicine: a photo of the label on the original container, showing the child’s full name, the exact dose and the directions. With it, no separate note from the doctor is needed.
Has the child taken this medication before?
The first dose is given at home. We may not give a medicine a child has never taken — unless it is an emergency medicine such as an inhaler or an epinephrine injector.
Ohio allows it only when the center has this permission on file.
