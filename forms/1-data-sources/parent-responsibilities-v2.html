Parent Responsibilities

  1. Make arrangements to pick up your child if they are sick, running a fever, or show any symptoms that we feel would require them to go home. Do not bring them back to the center until they are fever free for 24 hours without being on medication. Some exceptions can be made for this rule. Please check with the Director or the lead teacher.
  2. Notify the center if you change your phone numbers or your address.
  3. Pay all fees on time. Fees are due one week in advance.
  4. Complete all required forms on time. Most of our forms are only good for one year. It is your responsibility to get the forms updated in a timely manner when asked to by the administration. Please schedule your child’s yearly examination in a timely manner. Ask the Director for a Child Medical Statement so that they can update the child’s records.
  5. Notify the center if your child is going to be late or absent.
  6. Inform your child’s teacher about the child’s like and dislikes, fears, habits, and positive influences in his/her life.
  7. Notify the center if anyone other than the designated person is going to pick up your child. We will not release them to someone that we do not know, without a written note, phone call. We will also ask to see identification.
  8. Contact the Director if you have any questions or concerns.
  9. Check your child’s cubby for information about the program, notes from teachers, and other interests.
  10. Attend your child’s annual parent teacher conference.
