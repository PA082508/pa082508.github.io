Household Letter — Income Eligibility Guidelines

Dear Parent/Guardian,

Play Academy participates in the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP). Meals and snacks served to enrolled children may be claimed for reimbursement. Households may qualify their child for free or reduced-price meal benefits based on the federal income eligibility guidelines in effect for FY 2026–2027.

To apply for benefits, complete the Income Eligibility Application for Free and Reduced Price Meals and return it to your center. If your household income is above the reduced-price guidelines, or you choose not to apply, sign the Waiver on the previous page instead. Exactly one of the two — the application or the waiver — must be on file for each child.

Reviewing the current USDA Income Eligibility Guidelines (household size and annual/monthly/weekly income thresholds) will help you decide which form applies to your household. Your center director can provide the current guideline table and answer any questions.
