Play Academy · to every family, as the transition reaches you

Dear Play Academy Families,

Play Academy is moving from paper records to electronic ones. As the transition reaches your family, you will receive the necessary forms and a link with a QR code: it sets up the app for your child's drop-off and pick-up and opens your personal account, where you manage your family's information and stay in touch with Play Academy staff.

The transition is happening family by family. If you have received this letter, your family's turn has come — please complete the steps within one week.

Start by reading the Addendum to the Parent Handbook and signing to confirm you have read it.

Thank you!

— Play Academy
