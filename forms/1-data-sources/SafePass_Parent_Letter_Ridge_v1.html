Subject: New at Play Academy Ridge — SafePass drop-off & pick-up (Red Room starts Monday, August 10)

Dear Red Room families,

Starting Monday, August 10, Red Room becomes the first classroom at Play Academy to use SafePass — our own simple, secure way to check your child in and out.

One-time setup (about 2 minutes):

  1. At the door, Ms. Sonia will register you and take your photo — at pick-up, that photo is what tells the teacher "this is the right adult."
  2. On your own phone, open {LINK} (or scan the QR at the classroom door) and enter your phone number — the same one we have on file. No passwords, no codes, nothing to install. Tip: choose "Add to Home Screen."

Every day after: dropping off — tap Drop-off when you arrive; picking up — tap Pick-up; the teacher sees your photo on the classroom tablet, checks it's really you, and releases your child. The final check is always a person, face to face.

No smartphone? No problem — we'll set up a permanent personal QR card that works the same way at the door.

One request: please make sure at least two adults are authorized to pick up your child — a second parent, a grandparent, a trusted friend. It protects you on the day your phone dies or work runs late. Just tell Ms. Sonia who to add.

Questions? Ask Ms. Sonia at drop-off or reply to this message.

Thank you for helping us keep every hand-off safe.

— Play Academy Ridge
