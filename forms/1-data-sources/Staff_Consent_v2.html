Dear Team Member,

At Play Academy, Inc., we are streamlining onboarding and employment paperwork so you spend less time on forms. Some employee forms — such as your Staff Enrollment, job-description acknowledgments, the device-use (BYOD) agreement, and workplace policy acknowledgments — will use electronic signatures (e-signatures).

Electronically signed employment forms are acceptable. Your electronic signature carries the same intent and authorization as a handwritten signature on these forms.

By signing below, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • You consent to receiving and completing applicable employment forms electronically.
  • You understand that your electronic signature will be considered your legal signature on documents provided by Play Academy, Inc.
  • You may request a paper copy of any document at any time.
  • Signing this consent does not change the terms or at-will nature of your employment.

If you have any questions about this process, please contact the office. Thank you for being part of our team.

Sincerely,
Play Academy, Inc.
