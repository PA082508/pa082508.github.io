Standing Order

We are moving the daily child and employee attendance records into the tablet through SafePass and the Teacher App. This Standing Order explains what those records are, what is expected of you, and how recorded work time is used for payroll.

This system maintains accurate records of children's attendance and employees' work time while supporting the safety of the children in our care and accurate employee payment.

Please read this order carefully, ask any questions you have, and sign the acknowledgment at the end.

The Electronic Records

Beginning on the effective date, SafePass and the Teacher App will be the center's primary electronic records for:

  • Child Attendance — when a child is accepted into the center's care and when the child is released to an authorized person at pickup. The electronic child attendance record is an important safety record.
  • Employee work time — when an employee begins work, begins an unpaid meal break, returns from an unpaid meal break, and ends work.
  • Meal Count – CACFP requires timely meal recording.

The center may maintain a paper record as a backup when necessary. If a paper record is used because the electronic system is unavailable, the recorded times must accurately reflect the employee's actual work time and the child's actual time in care.

The center will maintain required employment and payroll records in accordance with applicable law.

Employee Responsibilities

  • Tap "Accept" a child only after the child is physically in your supervision.
  • Tap "Release" a child only after the child has been physically handed over to an authorized person.
  • Do not release the child if the pick-up person does not clearly match the photograph shown on the tablet.
  • Record meals while serving them, as required by the center's procedures.
  • Your PIN or login credentials are your individual electronic signature.
  • Do not share your PIN or login credentials with another employee.
  • Do not use another employee's PIN or login credentials.
  • If you are unable to access your account, notify your director rather than using another employee's account.
  • All employees, including directors and administrators, must follow the center's timekeeping procedures.
  • Each employee is expected to make four timekeeping entries when applicable:
  • Check In → Check out for Break → Check in from Break → Check Out
  • An unpaid meal break must be recorded using the appropriate Check Out and Check In entries.
  • If an employee leaves the classroom for work-related activities, they must tap "Step Out" so they are not used in the classroom ratio and are paid.
  • Employee time entries must be made using the center's designated timekeeping devices.
  • A personal phone is not an authorized time clock unless the center specifically authorizes it for that purpose.
  • The electronic time record is used together with the employee's approved schedule and other payroll records to determine the hours worked for payroll purposes.
  • Employees are responsible for accurately recording their actual work time.
  • Employees should not perform unauthorized work outside their scheduled hours. If additional work is necessary, employees should obtain authorization from a director whenever practicable.
  • Overtime, when applicable, will be handled in accordance with federal and Ohio law and the employee's applicable classification.
  • Accurate timekeeping protects the employee, the center, and the children in our care.
