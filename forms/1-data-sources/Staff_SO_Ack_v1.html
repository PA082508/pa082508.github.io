Standing Order

We are moving the daily record of two things — who takes a child, and when each of us works — onto the tablet, through SafePass and the Teacher App. This order explains what that record is, what it asks of you, and how pay follows it. It is written so that the record is fair to you and safe for the children. Please read it, ask any question you have, and sign it in person.

What the electronic record is

From the effective date, SafePass and the Teacher App are the official record of two things: handoffs (every time a child is accepted in the morning and released at pickup) and work time (every time you start work, step out for lunch, come back, and leave). The paper sheet still exists as a backup, and its times must match the tablet.

Your responsibilities at the door

  • Accept and Release are a legal record. Tap Accept only after the child is physically in your hands, and Release only after you have handed the child over.
  • A child who is not marked out is still, on the record, in our care — under your personal responsibility, whatever happened at the door. The door does not close a child's time in care; the Release mark does.
  • A child who is not marked in is the same duty without a record. An unanswered request distorts the child's recorded time in care.
  • The face on the screen must be the face at the door. If anything does not match, do not release — call your director.
  • Mark meals while the meal is being served.
  • Your PIN is your signature — do not share it, and do not work under anyone else's.

Your work time — four marks

Each shift you make four marks: Clock in · Break out · Break in · Clock out.

  • Lunch is not paid — the Break marks are your unpaid lunch. Other Step out reasons — Planning, Escorting a child, Kitchen, Other — are work time and are not deducted.
  • Everyone marks — including directors.
  • Marks are made on the center's devices only — a personal phone is never a time clock.
  • For roles outside the classroom, time marks take effect as the staff time station reaches your role — teachers are in full effect now.
  • If the tablet is broken, write your times on paper and give them to your director the same day.

How pay is calculated

  • Paid time is where your approved schedule and your actual marks overlap. Pay counts from your scheduled start and ends at your scheduled end.
  • Overtime happens only when a director changes your schedule in advance.
  • Time you are away within your scheduled hours is taken off by what you actually marked.
  • Pay runs on your own marks — accuracy protects your own paycheck.

This is a record of hours worked against the schedule, not a statement about anyone's pay rate. It is meant to be accurate and fair, in both directions.

Acknowledgment

Please acknowledge this order in person, on the tablet, with your PIN. Your signature says you have read it and understand it. The center keeps a signed copy in your personnel file, and you can see it there at any time.

This order is here to protect the children in our care and to keep an honest, fair record of everyone's work. Thank you for the care you give every day.
